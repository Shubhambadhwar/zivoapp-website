Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2024

Refund Duration: Refund requests must be raised within 7 days of the transaction date.

Refund Mode: Refunds are credited back to the original payment method (UPI / Bank Account / Card) within 5–7 working days after approval.

1. Eligible Refunds

You may be eligible for a refund in the following cases:

  • Coins were purchased but not credited to your wallet due to a technical error.
  • Duplicate payment was made for the same transaction.
  • Payment was deducted but the transaction was not completed (payment gateway failure).

2. Non-Refundable Cases

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Coins that have already been used to send gifts.
  • Coins spent on video calls (whether completed or not).
  • Refund requests raised after 7 days from the transaction date.
  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Account banned due to policy violations.

3. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email us at montybadhwar0720@gmail.com with subject: "Refund Request – [Your Registered Phone Number]"
  2. Include your transaction ID, date of purchase, and amount.
  3. Our team will review and respond within 3 working days.
  4. If approved, refund will be processed within 5–7 working days to your original payment method.

4. Refund Mode

Approved refunds are processed to:

  • UPI: Credited to the same UPI ID used during payment.
  • Card: Credited to the same Debit/Credit Card.
  • Net Banking: Credited to the same bank account.

Refunds are processed by PayU India and may take 5–7 working days depending on your bank.
