Our Services

ZIVO offers a range of digital services to creators and viewers on our live social platform.

1. Live Streaming

Hosts can broadcast live video to their followers in real time. Viewers can watch, comment, and interact during live sessions. Live streams are hosted securely on ZIVO's infrastructure.

2. Virtual Gift Sending

Viewers can purchase ZIVO Coins and use them to send virtual gifts to hosts during live streams. Each gift has a coin value. Hosts receive a portion of the gift value as earnings credited to their account.

3. Private Video Calls

Viewers can initiate one-on-one private video calls with available hosts. Calls are charged per minute based on the host's set rate. Coins are deducted from the viewer's wallet during the call.

4. ZIVO Coin Wallet

Every user has a digital coin wallet. Coins are a virtual in-app currency used to pay for gifts and calls. Coins are purchased via the in-app store using UPI, cards, or net banking (powered by PayU).

5. Host Earnings & Level System

Hosts earn coins through gifts and calls. Their profile level is automatically updated weekly based on earnings. Higher levels unlock better visibility and rewards.

6. Notifications

Push notifications are sent for incoming calls, received gifts, and platform updates to keep users engaged and informed.

Service Availability

ZIVO services are available 24/7 subject to network availability and scheduled maintenance. We are continuously improving our platform to deliver the best experience.

All services are digital in nature. No physical goods are delivered. Services are provided through the ZIVO mobile application available on Android.
